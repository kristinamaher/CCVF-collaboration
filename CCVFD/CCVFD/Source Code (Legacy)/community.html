Membership Information

Membership (previously called "subscriptions") helps support CCVFD operations and entitles you to emergency services coverage. Annual memberships are available to residents for a small fee.

What is a Membership

Clear Creek Volunteer Fire Department (CCVFD) is a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization. All of our Firefighters are unpaid volunteers who graciously volunteer their time to train, to respond, and to keep the organization functioning safely and with the best coverage they can provide. Most of our volunteers even have full-time jobs.

CCVFD is a non-profit, volunteer fire agency that relies on donations and membership dues. This means that CCVFD does not receive funding from annual property taxes, but instead must rely on donations, the occasional equipment grants, and annual membership payments from the community in order to continue providing fire fighting and related public safety services.

What does my Membership Pay For?

Subscriptions end up paying for things like:

  • Station maintenance & utility fees
  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
  • Vehicle fuel, maintenance, insurance, upgrades & compliance, and annual inspections
  • Equipment maintenance, repair, & inspections
  • Training materials and classes

Without this funding, the fire department would have to either change to a taxing district, or worst-case scenario, close its doors. Closing the doors would likely result in increased homeowners insurance costs, which are heavily tied to fire department response times and certifications. When you pay your subscription each year, it puts you in good standing. Beyond feeling good for supporting your local fire department, a member in good standing gets the following benefits:

  • Ability to vote in Board member elections. (This normally happens during our annual meeting)
  • We won't send you a bill if we end up on your property! Yes, the Fire Department is authorized by Idaho State Statute to issue an invoice to you and your insurance company to cover the costs of responding to an incident at your property.
  • To be clear: If you do not pay, that does not mean we will not respond to your property. It just means that you may get a bill if we respond and you are not a paid member.

Pay Membership Dues Today

Memberships are based on the following: First parcel is $75 per year. Each additional parcel is $75 if it has a habitable structure, or $10 if there are no habitable structures. You can pay your subscription by either mailing in your payment or paying through Paypal using the form below. If you would rather mail in your payment please ensure you include the reference number from your subscription (or your parcel number if you don't have the reference number).

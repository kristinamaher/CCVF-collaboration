Fall 2026 Burning Rules

Current local seasonal rule

Boise County's county closed season runs July 1 through October 20, unless adjusted by the Board of County Commissioners. Idaho's burn-permit requirement for most burning outside city limits runs May 10 through October 20.

Stage 1 restrictions in the Boise Fire Restriction Area were lifted September 3, 2026. That did not cancel Boise County's separate seasonal ban.

After October 20, continue checking current conditions before burning. Air-quality rules, land-manager restrictions, emergency orders, wind, fuel moisture, and local requirements can still make burning unsafe or unlawful.

Wildfire & Evacuation Preparedness

Around Your Home

  • Remove needles, leaves, and other easily ignited debris from roofs, gutters, decks, and the first five feet around structures.
  • Separate firewood, propane tanks, dead vegetation, and other fuels from buildings whenever practical.
  • Keep reflective address numbers visible from the road.
  • Maintain driveways and turnarounds for engines and water tenders.

Before an Evacuation

  • Register for Boise County emergency notifications.
  • Know your evacuation zone and at least two routes out.
  • Prepare medications, documents, chargers, clothing, pet supplies, and other essentials before an emergency.
  • Leave early when directed. Do not wait until fire is visible from your property.

Before Any Lawful Burn

  1. Confirm county, state, federal, land-manager, and air-quality rules allow burning.
  2. Obtain every required permit.
  3. Check wind, humidity, forecast, and red-flag status immediately before ignition.
  4. Have adequate water, hand tools, and responsible adult supervision on site.
  5. Never leave a burn unattended; remain until the fire is cold.
  6. Call 9-1-1 immediately if a fire escapes or conditions become unsafe.
